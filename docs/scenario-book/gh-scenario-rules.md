# Gloomhaven scenario rules — reference notes

What each of the 95 scenarios in the Gloomhaven scenario book asks for, in rules terms only: the
goal, how it can be lost besides exhaustion, set-up effects, objectives and escorts, what each
numbered section does, ongoing special rules, boss specials, and what each letter or number on
the map marks. Story text, requirements, rewards and unlocks are left out (the scenario data in
`GlavenGame/Resources/EditionData/gh/scenarios/` has those).

These are notes written from the book (second printing), not its text: every number, formula,
monster name and marker is as printed, the wording is ours. **L** is the scenario level, **C** the
number of characters. "(p.N)" is the page of the scenario book.

See [README.md](README.md) for what the game does with each of these today, and how hex
positions for the map letters are written.

### 1 · Black Barrow (p.3)
- **Goal:** Kill all enemies.
- **Map letters:** 1 = door from the corridor (G1b) into the treasure room (I1b); its section is story only.
- **Maps:** L1a, G1b, I1b

### 2 · Barrow Lair (p.4–5)
- **Goal:** Kill the Bandit Commander and all revealed enemies.
- **Setup:** Each character adds 3 Curse cards to their attack modifier deck (scenario effect).
- **Special rules:**
  - Doors a, b, c and d are locked; only the Bandit Commander can open them.
- **Boss specials:** Special 1: the Bandit Commander immediately jumps to a door hex, however far away, and opens it, revealing the room behind it; doors are taken in the order a, b, c, d, then back to a. Special 2: the Bandit Commander summons 1 normal Living Bones (2 characters) or 1 elite Living Bones (3–4 characters).
- **Map letters:** 1 = door from the start room (B3b) into the main crypt (M1a), section is story only; a = locked door M1a→A1a (right side room); b = locked door M1a→A4b (lower-right side room, holds the treasure tile); c = locked door M1a→A2a (lower-left side room); d = locked door M1a→A3b (left side room).
- **Maps:** B3b, M1a, A1a, A2a, A3b, A4b

### 3 · Inox Encampment (p.5–6)
- **Goal:** Kill a number of enemies equal to 5×C.
- **Special rules:**
  - Until door 1 is opened, 1 normal Inox Guard spawns at (a): at the end of every odd round (2 characters), or at the beginning of every round (3–4 characters).
- **Map letters:** 1 = door from the central clearing (L1b) into the back cave (E1b), section itself is story only but opening it stops the spawns; a = spawn hex on L1b, directly in front of door 1.
- **Maps:** L1b, L3a, B1a, B2a, B3a, B4a, E1b

### 19 · Forgotten Crypt (p.25–26)
- **Goal:** Protect Hail (a) until she reaches the altar (b); the scenario is complete when she ends her turn in a hex adjacent to the altar.
- **Lose:** Hail is killed.
- **Setup:** Each character adds 3 Curse cards to their attack modifier deck (scenario effect).
- **Objectives:** Hail (a), represented by a numbered token: 4+(2×L) HP; ally of the characters, enemy of all monster types; acts on initiative 99 every round, performing Move 2 toward the altar (b), opening doors and springing traps if necessary.
- **Section 1** (door 1 opened): spawn at (c): 1 normal Cultist (2 characters) or 2 normal Cultists (3–4 characters).
- **Section 2** (door 2 opened): spawn at (d): 1 normal Living Spirit (2–3 characters) or 2 normal Living Spirits (4 characters).
- **Section 3** (door 3 opened): spawn at (e): 1 normal Living Bones (2 characters) or 2 normal Living Bones (3–4 characters).
- **Map letters:** a = Hail's start (D2a, beside the character start hexes); b = altar (I1b); c = spawn hex on the start tile (D2a); d = spawn hex on D1a; e = spawn hex on C2b; 1 = door D1a→C2b; 2 = door C2b→C1a; 3 = door C1a→I1b. The door between D2a and D1a is unnumbered.

### 21 · Infernal Throne (p.28–29)
- **Goal:** Kill the Prime Demon.
- **Objectives:** Altar: shares hit points, initiative and immunities with the Prime Demon.
- **Section 1** (a door marked 1 opened, into the Prime Demon's room): story only.
- **Special rules:**
  - The Prime Demon cannot be damaged in any way; damage has to be dealt to the altar instead.
- **Boss specials:** Special 1: (1) the altar jumps to the next hex in the cycle a → b → c → d → e → f → a; a figure or overlay tile on the destination hex is moved to the nearest unoccupied hex; if the destination is in an unrevealed room, that room is revealed at once and every door between it and other revealed rooms opens. (2) The altar summons one demon — normal (2 characters) or elite (3–4) — of the type tied to its new location: a Frost Demon, b Wind Demon, c Night Demon, d Earth Demon, e Flame Demon, f Sun Demon. (3) The Prime Demon performs "Move+2, Attack-1". Special 2: same as Special 1, but the summoned demon is normal for 2–3 characters and elite for 4.
- **Map letters:** a–f = the six altar hexes (a, b on the lower J tile; c, d on the upper J tile; e on D2a; f on D1a). The rules text labels the altar "a", but the map draws the altar overlay on hex f (directly below the Prime Demon, in the room behind the doors marked 1); hex a is drawn as an empty hex. 1 = the two doors into the D1a room holding the Prime Demon.
- **Maps:** E1a, J1a, J2a, D1a, D2a

### 22 · Temple of the Elements (p.29–30)
- **Goal:** Destroy all altars (a).
- **Objectives:** Altar (a), ×4: 4+(C×L) HP each.
- **Section 1** (door 1 opened): story, then the altar rules below are printed.
- **Special rules:**
  - For every altar not yet destroyed (revealed or not), all demons get +1 maximum hit points and +1 attack, and +0.5 movement and +0.5 range (total rounded up).
  - Melee attacks gain no range from this.
- **Map letters:** a = the four altars, one in each corner room (top-left D2a with Earth Demons, top-right D1a with Flame Demons, bottom-left C1a with Frost Demons, bottom-right C2b with Wind Demons); 1 = door from the start room (B2b) into the central room (M1a).
- **Maps:** B2b, M1a, D1a, D2a, C1a, C2b

### 26 · Ancient Cistern (p.35–36)
- **Goal:** Cleanse all water pumps.
- **Setup:** Each character adds 3 Curse cards to their attack modifier deck (scenario effect).
- **Section 1** (door 1 opened): the pump rules below come into play.
- **Special rules:**
  - Number of water pumps equals C; all must be cleansed. 2 characters: pumps in the water hexes a; 3: a and b; 4: a, b and c.
  - A character adjacent to a pump may give up a bottom action (discarding that card instead) to cleanse it.

- **Map letters:** a = two water hexes (pumps) at the far end of the last room (L1a); b, c = one water hex each (pumps) near the entrance of that room (L1a); 1 = door from J1a into the C1a room, which joins the pump room (L1a) through an open two-hex corridor with no door.

### 27 · Ruinous Rift (p.37)
- **Goal:** Protect Hail (a) for ten rounds.
- **Lose:** Hail is killed.
- **Objectives:** Hail (a), a numbered token: 4+(2×L) HP; ally, enemy of all monster types; initiative 99 for monster focus purposes only.

- **Map letters:** a = Hail's hex (centre, next to the altar, among the start hexes); b = bottom-right corner spawn hex; c = top-right; d = top-left; e = bottom-left (all M1a).
- **Maps:** M1a

### 34 · Scorched Summit (p.45)
- **Goal:** Kill the Elder Drake.
- **Objectives:** Zephyrs (numbered tokens, summoned by the boss): cannot be damaged in any way; each round, immediately before the Elder Drake's turn, they perform "Move 2, Attack C".
- **Special rules:**
  - The Elder Drake occupies all three hexes of the obstacle it sits on.
  - A figure that can target any one of those hexes can target the Elder Drake; the Elder Drake may use any of its hexes to work out what it can target.
  - The Elder Drake cannot be forced to move or have its position changed in any way.
  - The huge boulder obstacles cannot be destroyed.
- **Boss specials:** Special 1: nothing printed in the scenario book. Special 2: the Elder Drake summons two Zephyrs, then flies to the next boulder perch in the cycle a → b → c → a.
- **Map letters:** a = huge-boulder perch on the right; b = huge-boulder perch on the left; c = huge-boulder perch at the top centre, where the Elder Drake is drawn at setup.
- **Maps:** L2a, L3b

### 35 · Gloomhaven Battlements A (p.46–47)
- **Goal:** Destroy door 1 and kill the Captain of the Guard.
- **Setup:** Place the City Archers grouped at a and b at the start. The other enemies on the B and I tiles are not set up until door 1 is destroyed or a character or character ally moves onto tile B or I; from then on normal room-reveal rules apply.
- **Objectives:** Door 1: (7+L)×C HP.
- **Section 1** (door 1 destroyed): story only.
- **Special rules:**
  - Door 1 is locked and opens when it is destroyed.
  - All demons are allies of the characters and enemies of all other monster types; they still act normally from a monster ability card each round.
  - On a demon's turn, if it can move to within range to attack the door, it focuses on the door and takes its turn normally; otherwise it picks its focus by the normal rules.
- **Map letters:** a = group of two City Archers on the left battlement; b = group of two City Archers on the right battlement; 1 = the gate door between the outside tiles (L1b, L3a) and the inside tiles (I1b on the left, B2b on the right).
- **Maps:** L1b, L3a, I1b, B2b

### 36 · Gloomhaven Battlements B (p.47–48)
- **Goal:** Kill the Prime Demon.
- **Setup:** Do not set up the Prime Demon. City Archers are set up at the start of the scenario.
- **Objectives:** Door 1: 10+(2×L) HP; cannot be healed; not considered an ally of the characters.
- **Section 1** (door 1 destroyed): story only (the Prime Demon's arrival at e is covered by the special rules).
- **Special rules:**
  - The Prime Demon spawns at e only once door 1 is destroyed.
  - The Prime Demon starts the scenario with twice the hit points shown on its stat card.
  - At the end of each round in which door 1 is not yet destroyed, the Prime Demon suffers (2×C)+L-2 damage.
  - Door 1 is locked and opens when it is destroyed by demons.
  - On a demon's turn, if it can move to within range to attack the door, it focuses on the door and takes its turn normally; otherwise it picks its focus by the normal rules.
  - All City Archers are allies of the characters and enemies of all other monster types; they still act normally from a monster ability card each round.
  - At the end of each round one demon spawns, starting at a and cycling a → b → c → d → a. Type by hex: a Flame Demon, b Earth Demon, c Frost Demon, d Wind Demon.
  - Spawn rank: all normal for 2 characters; for 3 characters Earth and Wind Demons normal, Flame and Frost Demons elite; all elite for 4.
  - At the end of round 8, after the Prime Demon has suffered its damage, door 1 is destroyed automatically if it is still standing.
- **Boss specials:** Ignore the specials on the Prime Demon's stat card; for each special (1 and 2) it performs "Move+0, Attack+0" instead.
- **Map letters:** a = spawn hex on the left edge of the outside area (L3a); b = spawn hex at the top of the left outside tile (L3a); c = spawn hex at the top of the right outside tile (L1b); d = spawn hex on the right edge (L1b); e = Prime Demon's spawn hex outside the gate, just beyond the row of start hexes in front of door 1; 1 = the gate door between the outside tiles and the inside tiles (I1b, B2b).
- **Maps:** L1b, L3a, I1b, B2b

### 41 · Timeworn Tomb (p.54–55)
- **Goal:** All characters must escape through the exit (a).

### 42 · Realm of the Voice (p.56)
- **Goal:** Destroy all vocal chords.
- **Objectives:** Six vocal chords, numbered tokens 1–6: each has 2+(L×C/2) HP, rounded down.
- **Special rules:** While a given chord is alive its penalty is in force:
  - Chord 1: all monsters add +1 Attack to all their attacks.
