Fixes and polish from playing beta 3 on the Mac and iPad.

- **The Mac's home screen fills the window at once.** On first launch it used to start squeezed into a corner and slowly grow.
- **Learning tips stay clear of the title bar** on the Mac, and of the status bar on the iPad.
- **An ability that can't do anything says why**, above your played cards, for example "Rock Tunnel: no obstacle beside you". Before, the only sign was a line in the log.
- **Quit** on the Mac's home screen.
- **Settings during a scenario**, from the board's menu. Card style and size, text size and the rest change at once.
- **Turn a magnified card** to see it as the printed scan or as the drawn card.
- **Drawn cards read better on the iPad**: an area sits beside its attack, long marks wrap, and crowded halves shrink only as far as they must.
- **No road event before a random dungeon**, since it doesn't progress the campaign.
