**Glaven 1.0**: the first full release of a digital Gloomhaven for the Mac.

Every one of the 95 campaign scenarios plays with all of its printed rules, the monsters run themselves by the rulebook and FAQ, and the town and campaign carry on between scenarios.

## New since beta 4

- **It's called Glaven now.** The app was "GlavenGame". Your saves, settings and campaigns carry over.
- **A new app icon**, the crest, plus four others to choose from in Settings. On the Mac your choice shows in the Dock while Glaven is open; on the iPad it's on the Home Screen, in light, dark and tinted.

## What's in Glaven

- **All 95 scenarios**, each with its own goal, losses and special rules, plus random dungeons from the random dungeon deck.
- **Monsters that play by the book**: focus, movement, area attacks, bosses' special rules, and summons and allies on their own turns.
- **Your turn your way**: two cards a round, every printed action performed step by step, items, persistent bonuses, elements, rests, and losing cards to negate damage.
- **The campaign**: town with the shop, the Enhancer and the Sanctuary, city and road events, levelling up, perks, personal quests, retirement and new classes, battle goals, and achievements on the world map. Several campaigns can be saved side by side.
- **Cards you can read**: the printed scans or drawn cards in plain type, at the size you like, and full size when you magnify one.
- **Learning as you go**: a learning mode that explains what's happening, and an illustrated How to Play.
- **Sound, VoiceOver and larger text** throughout.
