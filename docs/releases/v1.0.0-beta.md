The first public beta of Glaven: Gloomhaven on the Mac, played on a hex board with the monsters run for you.

## What's in it

- **The board**: the scenario's map tiles, doors and overlays; characters moved and abilities played by tapping hexes and figures.
- **Monsters that play by the rules**: focus, movement and attacks from their ability cards, including area attacks, push and pull, and summoning.
- **Combat**: attack modifier decks with Bless and Curse (ten of each at most), advantage, shield, pierce, retaliate and conditions.
- **Movement**: jumping and flying shown as such, teleport, traps that apply their conditions, hazardous terrain, and obstacles and traps placed during a scenario.
- **Your turn**: persistent ability cards kept in play, items spent and consumed, and loot picked up at the end of your turn.
- **Escorts and objectives**: escorts walk and fight on their own, and objectives appear in their numbers.
- **The campaign**: city and road events, personal quests and battle goals checked for you, bonus experience by scenario level, treasure rewards, and achievement stickers on the world map.

macOS only. This is a beta: expect rough edges, and keep in mind saves may not carry into later versions.
