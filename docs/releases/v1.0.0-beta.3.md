The big one: Glaven now plays by the Gloomhaven rulebook, its FAQ and the scenario book. **All 95 scenarios** have every printed rule, the random dungeon deck is playable, and the town and campaign run between scenarios. (Beta 2 was never published; its fixes are in here too.)

## Scenarios as the book prints them

- Objectives, escorts and lettered hexes placed from the scenario book, and each scenario's own goal and ways to lose.
- Locked doors and pressure plates, and traps that do what each scenario's legend says.
- Standing effects and boss specials: the Infernal Throne's altar, the Elder Drake's perches, the Betrayer's mind control.
- Things carried, like the doll, the claw and the Staff of Xorn.
- Boards that move as the round ends: wind, current, rolling boulders, rising water.
- Monsters that don't act as their cards say: sleeping drakes, a siege cannon, cultists carrying their victim.
- Walls and obstacles with hit points.
- **Random dungeons**: the deck's 20 room cards and 20 monster cards, rooms drawn by the deck's rules, treasure tiles, and room penalties at a difficulty you choose. #55 Foggy Thicket plays as one.

## Choices are yours

- Lose a card to spare a captive.
- Give up an action to free a captive or loot a treasure.
- Pick the element for an "any element" bonus.
- Aim area attacks.
- Choose which shield comes first.

## Classes, cards and items

- Every printed action of a card is performed step by step.
- Charged and persistent bonuses.
- The Doomstalker's dooms, and the Beast Tyrant's bear and Commands.
- Dozens of items played on your turn and on others'.

## Town and campaign

- City and road events, the shop at the city's prices, levelling up with a card and a perk.
- The Enhancer (summon stats included), the Sanctuary, battle goals and personal quests, retirement and new classes.
- Travel on to the next scenario without returning to Gloomhaven.
- Collective gold split between players.
- Several campaigns, each saved to its own file.

## Cards you can read

- Choose between the printed card scans and drawn cards that say everything a card prints in plain type.
- A slider sizes your cards in hand and in play.
- Magnify a card to read it full size.

## The board

- A new layout, portrait tokens, and wall lines between tiles.
- Attack previews and a turn-order rail.
- A learning mode and an illustrated How to Play.
- Sound, VoiceOver throughout, and larger text.

## Fixes

- Rounds, conditions, monster decks and turns reworked to the rulebook and FAQ.
- Line of sight, range, and push and pull count around walls.
- Damage from any source can be negated by losing cards.
- Saves keep everything and load older ones.
- Board hangs found in long unattended runs are fixed.
